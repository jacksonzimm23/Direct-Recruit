Transfer Portal Database

Welcome to the Transfer Portal Database

The Transfer Portal Database allows student-athletes to make their name available to other programs while looking to transfer.

Important: The Transfer Portal Database is only for NCAA athletes with 1+ years of remaining eligibility. Please ensure you meet all requirements before proceeding.

Pre-Transfer Checklist

Complete all items before entering the Transfer Portal Database

Notify Current School Compliance
Contact your school's compliance office to notify them of your intent to transfer
Discussed with Current Football Staff
Have a conversation with your current coaching staff about your decision to transfer
Checked Transfer Portal Window Dates
Verify that the transfer window is currently open and note the closing date
Completed NCAA Transfer Module
Watch the required NCAA Transfer Module video and obtain your completion certificate
Checklist Progress 0 of 4 completed

This action will make your name available to other programs.
