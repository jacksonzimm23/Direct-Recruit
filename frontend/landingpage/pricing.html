Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial for coach plans?

We offer a Starter plan at $20/month so you can try the AI tools at a lower commitment before upgrading.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards. Payments are processed securely through Stripe.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep access through the end of your billing period. Your profile and data are never deleted — you can resubscribe anytime.

Do college players have to pay?

No. College players, HS coaches, parents, and fans get full free accounts. Only HS players, transfer players, and college coaches with AI tools have paid plans.

Is there a student discount?

The $10/month player plan is already priced for students. We may offer additional discounts in the future.
