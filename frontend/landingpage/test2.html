The Challenge

The Problem We're Solving

College sports recruiting has become unnecessarily complicated and inefficient

🔍

Lost in the Noise

Social media platforms are cluttered with unrelated content, making it nearly impossible for coaches to find talent and for players to get noticed.

🎲

Luck-Based Discovery

Finding the right recruit or the right coach shouldn't depend on a lucky scroll or random message. The current system leaves too much to chance.

⏰

Time-Consuming Process

Coaches waste countless hours sifting through irrelevant content, while talented players go unnoticed despite their potential.

📱

Fragmented Communication

Recruiting conversations are scattered across multiple platforms, making it difficult to maintain organized and professional communication.

How the Transfer Portal Works

College athletes can opt-in to the transfer portal, making their profile visible to coaches from other programs who are actively recruiting transfers.

Why Use Our Transfer Portal?

  • Privacy control - You decide when you're visible to coaches
  • Targeted exposure - Only serious coaches actively recruiting transfers
  • Organized process - Keep all transfer communications in one place
  • Updated profiles - Showcase your college stats and experience
  • Multiple opportunities - Connect with programs across all divisions
