Recruiting Deserves
Its Own Platform.

Direct Recruit connects athletes with college coaches through a platform built specifically for recruiting. Not bolted onto a social network. Built from scratch.

Why We Built This

College recruiting is broken in ways everyone knows but nobody has fixed.

Talent gets buried

Social media is cluttered with unrelated content. Coaches can't find players, and players can't get noticed no matter how good they are.

Discovery depends on luck

Finding the right recruit or the right program shouldn't depend on a lucky scroll or knowing the right person. The system leaves too much to chance.

Communication is scattered

Recruiting conversations happen across Instagram DMs, Twitter, email, and text. Messages get lost. Opportunities get missed.

No tools built for this

Coaches need to search, filter, and organize recruits. Players need to know who's watching. None of the existing platforms were built for that.

What Players Get

Everything you need to get discovered and build real connections with coaches.

Recruiting Profile

Stats, highlights, film, academics, and offers. Everything a coach needs to evaluate you, in one place.

AI Rep

See which coaches viewed your profile, what they searched for, and get personalized tips to increase your exposure.

Highlight Film

Upload unlimited highlight videos. Coaches can watch your film directly from your profile without leaving the platform.

Direct Messaging

Message college coaches directly. No more DMs lost in spam. A dedicated inbox coaches actually check.

Discover Coaches

Find programs across D1, D2, D3, NAIA, and JUCO. Filter by state, division, and position need.

Social Feed

Post updates, share highlights, and engage with the recruiting community. Every interaction is about recruiting.

What Coaches Get

AI powered tools built to save time and find the right players for your program.

AI Recruiting Assistant

Search for players in plain English. "Find me a 6'2+ QB from Texas with a 3.5+ GPA" and get instant, ranked results.

Portal Intelligence

Detect transfer portal signals before players announce. Track bio changes, film uploads, and coach browsing behavior.

Recruiting Board

Organize recruits by position and class year in a kanban style board. Share boards with your coaching staff.

Advanced Search

Filter players by position, size, academics, location, stats, film, offers, and eligibility. Find exactly who you need.

Saved Players & Watchlists

Save players from search results for later review. Build a portal watchlist to monitor players who may transfer.

Direct Messaging

Reach out to recruits directly. Real time conversations built for recruiting, not buried in social media DMs.

Transfer Portal Integration

College players can enter the portal and control their visibility. Coaches can search and filter transfer players by position, eligibility, and division.

For players entering the portal

Control when you're visible to coaches. Showcase your college stats and experience. Connect with programs across all divisions on your terms.

For coaches searching the portal

Search transfer players by position, eligibility, previous division, and stats. Use Portal Intelligence to detect signals before players even announce.

Our Mission

Direct Recruit exists to democratize college sports recruiting. Every talented athlete deserves to be seen by coaches, and every coach deserves efficient tools to find the right players for their program. We're removing the noise and luck from the process and replacing it with real connections.
