Recruiting Calendar

What is Recruiting?

The NCAA defines recruiting as any solicitation of prospective student-athletes or their parents by an institutional staff member or representative of the institution's athletics interests for the purpose of securing enrollment and participation in intercollegiate athletics.

Recruiting Period Definitions

Contact Period
Coaches may call, text, email, watch prospects compete, visit their high school, and have face-to-face contact with prospects and their families anywhere.
Evaluation Period
Coaches may call, text, email, watch prospects compete, and visit their high school. Face-to-face contact is permitted only on the college's campus.
Quiet Period
Coaches may call, text, and email. Face-to-face contact is allowed only on the college's campus. Coaches may not watch prospects compete or visit their high school.
Dead Period
Coaches may only call, text, or email. No in-person contact of any kind - no campus visits, no watching competition, no high school visits.

Key Reminders

  • Certain recruiting activities may have division- and sport-specific restrictions before certain grade levels in high school.
  • A "contact" occurs any time a coach says more than hello during a face-to-face encounter with a prospect off campus.
  • Off-campus recruiters must be certified annually on applicable recruiting rules before recruiting off campus.
  • Once a prospect signs a written offer of athletics aid with another D1 or D2 institution, you may not communicate with the prospect or anyone associated with them.

D2 Recruiting Rules

Recruiting Materials
May be sent at any time, including to freshmen and sophomores.
Off-Campus Contacts
No limits after June 15 immediately preceding the prospect's junior year.
Two-Year College Transfers
No limits on recruiting materials, communication, or in-person off-campus contacts.
Four-Year College Transfers
Communication and in-person contacts allowed. Must comply with Transfer Portal rules when contacting D1 or D3 student-athletes.
After Signing
Once a prospect signs athletics aid with another D1 or D2 institution, all contact must cease - including with family, coaches, and advisors.

Key Notes

No Formal Periods
Unlike NCAA divisions, the NAIA does not enforce dead, quiet, or evaluation periods.
Typical Timeline
While contact is allowed earlier, most NAIA recruiting activity begins during a student's junior year.
JUCO Transfer Rule
As of April 7, 2025, NAIA coaches may initiate contact with junior college student-athletes at any time, removing previous restrictions.
