One score, seen from

A launch film for AI Match. A player answers a few quick questions and sees the schools that fit them. Flip the camera, and the same player shows up for the coach — ranked by fit, with the reasons why. The moral: recruiting works when both sides share the same off-field goals.

10 scenes ~67s target 2 new UI builds 2 bookends reused Voiceover-led
Blue = player's side Orange = coach's side Neutral = brand / transition
Framing (locked, accuracy-critical). The product says “two-way fit,” not “mutual interest.” AI Match scores the overlap of stated preferences — a player wants X, a program offers X. It is not a signal that a specific coach wants a specific player. The VO says fit / shared goals / who fits your program — never “this coach is interested in you.”

S4 The quick questionnaire 17.0–29.0sNew UI
Shows

A centered player-side card. A short fit questionnaire fills in fast — a cerulean progress bar racing to 100%, a checkmark ticking on each answer:

  • Academics — GPA + test chips fill in (GPA 3.6, SAT 1180)
  • Level — pills select D2 · D3
  • Region — Southeast · South Central light up

  • Priorities — a slider snaps (playing time ⟷ competition level)

Keep it brisk — the payoff is the matches, not the form. End on a satisfying 100% state.

VO “It starts with a few quick questions — academics, level, location, and what matters to you.”
S5 Matches resolve → Best-Fit + who to contact 29.0–39.0sNew UI
