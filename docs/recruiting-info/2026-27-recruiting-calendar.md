# 2026-27 NCAA Football Recruiting Calendar (Reference)

**Calendar cycle:** Aug 1, 2026 – July 31, 2027
**Last updated:** July 15, 2026 (7/15/26)
**Source:** Official NCAA Recruiting Calendars & FAQ — https://www.ncaa.org/sports/2021/2/10/recruiting-calendars-faq.aspx

> Only D1 FBS, D1 FCS, and D2 changed for 2026-27. D3, NJCAA, and NAIA are unchanged (they have no date-driven periods).

> **Design rule for this cycle:** periods are recorded exactly as the PDFs mark them. Uncovered dates are **not** auto-filled with a Contact Period. For D2, the year is now fully covered by explicit Contact periods, so there are no gaps. For D1 FBS/FCS, dates after the last listed period (after Dec 31) are intentionally uncovered.

Reference PDFs for this cycle should be saved in `docs/recruiting-info/screenshots/` as:
- `2026-27 D1 FBS Recruiting Calendar.pdf`
- `2026-27 D1 FCS Recruiting Calendar.pdf`
- `2026-27 D2 Recruiting Guide.pdf`
- `2026-27 D2 Recruiting Reminders.pdf`

(The unversioned PDFs already in that folder are the prior 2025-26 cycle — keep both.)
